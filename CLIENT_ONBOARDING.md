### 2. Your Options (customize for your business)

- [ ] Cities you serve (e.g., Riyadh, Jeddah, Dammam)
- [ ] Budget ranges (e.g., Under 500K, 500K-1M)
- [ ] Bedroom options (e.g., 1-2, 3-4, 5+)

### 3. WhatsApp Business Setup

You need a WhatsApp Business API account.

#### Step 1: Create Meta Business Account
1. Go to https://business.facebook.com
2. Click "Create Account"
3. Enter business name, your name, email
4. Verify your email

#### Step 2: Create Developer App
1. Go to https://developers.facebook.com
2. Click "My Apps" → "Create App"
3. Select "Business" → Next
4. Enter app name → Select your Business Account → Create

#### Step 3: Add WhatsApp
1. In app dashboard, click "Add Product"
2. Find "WhatsApp" → "Set Up"
3. Click "Start using the API"
4. Add phone number (must NOT be on regular WhatsApp)
5. Verify via SMS

#### Step 4: Get Credentials
Send us these:
- **Phone Number ID** (WhatsApp > API Setup)
- **Access Token** (Generate with no expiry)

## FAQ

**Q: Can I use my existing WhatsApp number?**
A: No, you need a new number not registered on regular WhatsApp.

**Q: How long does setup take?**
A: 3-5 business days after receiving payment and information.

**Q: Can I see my leads?**
A: Yes, real-time access to Google Sheet with all leads.

**Q: What if I want to cancel?**
A: Cancel anytime. No refunds for partial months.

**Q: What do I pay to Meta/WhatsApp?**
A: First 1,000 conversations/month are free. After that, small per-conversation fee paid directly to Meta.
